Privacy policy

Cutoff

Cutoff does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

Your bedtime, drinks and settings are stored only on your device. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone, and the Lock Screen countdown is a Live Activity on your phone.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.
