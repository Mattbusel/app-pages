Privacy policy

Rooms

Rooms does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

Your inventory, policy details and photos are stored only on your device. Photos taken with the camera or chosen from your library are saved inside the app's own folder and are never uploaded anywhere. The PDF and spreadsheet you make are shared only where you choose to send them.
