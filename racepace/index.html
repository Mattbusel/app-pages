Race Pace

Running pace calculator. Enter a distance and goal time for your pace, every split, race predictions, fueling, a printable pace band and a race-day clock.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email mattbusel@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from the developer.

Race Pace does arithmetic on your times. Predictions and fueling suggestions are general guidance, not medical advice.

Race Pace is a free download with one optional in-app purchase, Race Pace Pro, a one-time unlock. To restore it on a new phone, tap Restore purchase on the Pro card inside the app.
