Privacy policy

Parked

Parked does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

Your parking spots, photos, notes, garages and street rules are stored only on your device. Your location is read only while you park or look for your car, and never leaves your phone. To show a street name, the app asks Apple's system geocoder, under Apple's privacy policy. Reminders are local notifications.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.
