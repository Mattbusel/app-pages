Parked

Parking timer and car finder. Save where you parked, photograph the sign, run the meter, and get told when to head back, allowing for the walk.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email mattbusel@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from the developer.

Parked does not pay for parking or guarantee you won't get a ticket. Always read the posted signs.

Parked is a free download with one optional in-app purchase, Parked Pro, a one-time unlock. To restore it on a new phone, tap Restore purchase on the Pro card inside the app.
