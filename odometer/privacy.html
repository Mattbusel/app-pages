Privacy policy

Odometer

Odometer does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

Your vehicles, readings, services, fill-ups and expenses are stored only on your device. Optional reminders are local notifications that never leave the phone. The service history PDF and CSV export are created on the device and shared only where you choose to send them.
