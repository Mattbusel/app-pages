Privacy policy

Minder

Minder does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

Your focus minutes and eye settings are stored only on your device.

Camera. If you turn on "Follow my face" (it is off by default), the front camera is used during a work session to find where your face is in each frame, so the eyes can look toward you. Frames are processed on the device and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Motion. During a work session the app reads device motion to notice when the phone is picked up. Nothing is stored.
