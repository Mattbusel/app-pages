Privacy policy

Pricebook

Pricebook does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

Your items, prices, stores and shopping list are stored only on your device. The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read barcodes on the device; no pictures are saved or sent anywhere. The CSV export goes only where you choose to share it.
