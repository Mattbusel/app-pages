Pocket Beings

Seven tiny people. One town. No supervision.

Support

Something broken, or an idea? Email mattbusel@gmail.com and mention which iPhone you are on.

How to play

Tap NEW TOWN. Every couple of seconds somebody does something, and it shows up in the newspaper. Tap anyone in the yard to open their file and give, rob, jail, crown or exile them. Close the app; the town carries on, and when you come back the paper tells you what you missed.

Questions

Can I have more than one town? One at a time. NEW TOWN replaces the old one.

Why is everything so expensive? Whoever holds the Crown has been printing money. Exile them.

Do I need an internet connection? No. Nothing is collected and nothing leaves the phone.
