Chores

A chore chart, allowance bank and reward shop for families. Kids tap to finish chores, earn stars and pocket money, and spend them in the reward shop. Pay once, no subscription.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email mattbusel@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from the developer.

Forgot the parent PIN? Delete and reinstall the app to start fresh (this also erases the family's data), or email for help.
