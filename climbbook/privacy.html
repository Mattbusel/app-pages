Privacy policy

Climbbook

Climbbook does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers.

Your climbs, sessions, notes and photos are stored only on your device. Photos are taken or picked only when you choose to add one. The spreadsheet export goes only where you choose to share it.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.
